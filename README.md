## Overview
**Parallax Paradox** is an experimental optical-illusion puzzle game where players find themselves trapped in a surreal dreamscape resembling a mysterious museum filled with impossible architecture and reality-bending exhibits.

Each of the four themed exhibits contains five rooms, each challenging how players perceive and interact with space through:
- Forced perspective
- Portals
- 2D-to-3D transformations

Scattered digital alarm clocks hint that this world is a subconscious trial — the only escape is to solve every puzzle.

## Puzzle Mechanics / Features
- **Forced Perspective Scaling** – Objects change size based on appearance when picked up/dropped.
- **Portals** – Allow players to shift between different spaces/rooms.
- **Anamorphic 2D/3D Conversion** – 2D images align from certain perspectives and transform into 3D objects.
- **Pressure Pads** – Classic activation mechanic, often combined with other illusions.

---

## Core Gameplay Loop
1. **Enter/Explore Chamber** – Enter a new exhibit room.
2. **Observe the Environment** – Scan the room layout and identify potential illusions.
3. **Experiment** – Test scaling, portal behavior, and 2D illusions.
4. **Solve the Spatial Puzzle** – Use mechanics in combination to progress.
5. **Trigger Room Exit** – Advance to the next room or level.
6. **Master Mechanics** – Final room is an “infinite hallway” escape sequence blending prior elements.

**Simplified Loop:**  
`Explore → Observe → Experiment → Solve → Exit → Advance`

## Puzzle Solutions

### Level 1 – Artifacts Out of Scale
- **Room 1** – Use scaling on chess pieces to create platforms.
- **Room 2** – Scale down the door to progress.
- **Room 3** – Use the pressure pad to unlock the door.
- **Room 4** – Place the scalable skull on the pad.
- **Room 5 (Infinite Hallway)** – Use scalable horse on pad to unlock exit.

### Level 2 – Portals Through Time
- **Room 1** – Use portal and scale down the blocking door.
- **Room 2** – Use portals to collect props as platforms.
- **Room 3** – Place scalable telephone on pad.
- **Room 4** – Use scalable CRT TV to unlock door.
- **Room 5 (Infinite Hallway)** – Transport scalable walkman to pad.

### Level 3 – Perspective Pending
- **Room 1** – Spawn scalable Rubik’s Cube via anamorphic painting.
- **Room 2** – Place scalable shelf on pad.
- **Room 3** – Use painting to spawn portals.
- **Room 4** – Spawn scalable office monitor, place on pad.
- **Room 5 (Infinite Hallway)** – Transport scalable bucket to pad.

### Level 4 – Everything Everywhere, Perceived All at Once
- **Infinite Corridor** – Turn around, shrink door to progress.
- **Room 1** – Use portal to get scalable telephone, place on pad.
- **Room 2** – Use portal for scalable chair & Rubik’s Cube, open doors in sequence.
- **Room 3** – Spawn monitor via painting, use as platform and pad item.
- **Room 4** – Chain portals and props to unlock first section door.
- **Room 5 (Infinite Hallway)** – Combine portals, painting, and props to unlock door.

## Limitations / Bugs
- **Teleporting with Scalable Props** – Offset may prevent props from entering teleporters.
- **Thin Wall Collisions** – Oversized props may phase through thin walls.
- **Scaling Push-Out Glitch** – Extreme scaling can push player out of bounds.
- **Portal Texture Loading Issue** – Portals require both cameras in line of sight to render properly.
